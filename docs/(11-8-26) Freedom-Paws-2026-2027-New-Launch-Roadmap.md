## Advisor’s opening

This is the right plan for a durable company. It is not the plan that produces a mega company.

A mega outcome here would mean a default tool for shelters and owners across the country, with a team that no longer depends on you and a value well beyond the roughly $3 million at month 48 in the planning case. This plan is built to reach a real, profitable membership business on one person’s time and a $1,500 monthly cap. That is a serious success. It is a different assignment from “mega.”

**Why this sequence is the best one you can run under the rules you set.** You have a wide product, no customers, and a legal ceiling that forbids diagnosis. The cash register has to exist before ads, or you will pay to acquire people you cannot recognize on a second phone. The annual membership is the only offer in the current product that can compound. Affiliates are extra margin, not the company. The shelter seat is the distribution door, and its fee is the small line. SuperBud, the photo booth, a scanner you stock, and ViT Pro all spend time or cash before they create a second-year renewal. Leaving them dark is the scale decision, not a delay of it.

**The one place the plan is too small, if the product is actually good.** The booked rate is about two new members per paid shelter per month. The mechanism you described — every incoming dog enrolled at intake, the adopter claiming that record — is the only path in this company that can beat ads. A municipal shelter that adopts out 40 dogs a month and gets one in four adopters to claim the record is ten members a month from one organization. That is the mega wedge, and it is unmeasured. Until one Tennessee shelter, municipal or rescue, runs that loop for 90 days, every national number in the plan is a story. If the claim rate is real, the plan should be rewritten upward and the first hire should be someone who onboards shelters, not someone who makes more product. If the claim rate is near zero, no amount of SuperBud or hardware will scale it, and the membership-plus-small-ads path is the whole company.

**What not to do in the name of scale.** Do not raise money before that 90-day claim rate exists. Capital spent on ads, a studio, or a scanner inventory would buy a bigger version of a loop you have not seen work. Do not add a medical claim to chase a grant or a higher price. The companies that become large in pet health either own the clinic relationship or own a device people already wear. You own a record and a handoff. That can be large. It becomes fragile the moment the scan is sold as a diagnosis.

The best scaled approach, stated as a rule: finish the membership so a stranger can pay, then put every spare week into one shelter’s intake-to-claim rate. Scale shelters only after that rate is real. Keep the rest of the plan exactly as written until that number comes in. Mega, if it is available to this company at all, starts there. It does not start with a bigger build.

## What holds public mode

Public mode is the Vercel setting `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_MODE=public`. It allows search indexing and removes the preview banner. Preview mode disallows all robots and shows “Private preview — not indexed.”

Counsel is no longer what holds that switch. The October 26 note says selling, public mode, and the shop wait for the founder’s notice that the EIN is opened and the bank account is open. That notice has not been given.

Besides the EIN and the bank, public mode waits on these:

1. A **$1 Stripe charge that lands in the Freedom Paws Wellness LLC account.** A test charge must not hit a personal account.
2. A **membership that works on a second device**, written by the webhook, tied to the account. This replaces the October 31 line that accepted “a membership or one protocol unlock.” A protocol flag in the browser does not pass.
3. A **Framer check** the same week. Before the membership is live, every button lands on a free job: watch, share, the free scan, or enroll. After the second-device test passes, the membership checkout may be linked. Affiliate links stay off. The 10% sentence stays off.
4. The **founder notice** that the EIN and the bank are actually open. The clerk note does not open public mode by itself.
5. The **iPhone photo path.** On October 31, 2026, Direct Upload and `/vit-upload.html` completed the upload and then could not load the photo, because Vercel temporary storage is not shared across instances. The dashed-box upload that reads the file in the browser worked. A public ViT invite uses that path.

Do not flip the switch on the day the bank login arrives. Flip it after the $1 is in the LLC and the second-device membership works. That flip is planned for December 2026, in the same week founding checkout turns on.

# Introduction & Mission

## Welcome to Freedom Paws Wellness

For over 30 years, I have worked as a chiropractor and functional healthcare specialist, helping both people and their beloved dogs find natural paths to better health and vitality. My own dog, Buddy — a pug — became my greatest teacher. At 12 years old, he was diagnosed with mast cell cancer: a small bump that grew into a two-inch tumor. My veterinarian gave him only four to six months to live.

Through a whole-food diet, targeted natural supplements, gentle movement, and filling his days with love, Buddy not only beat the odds but thrived joyfully for **two full extra years**. Buddy’s remarkable journey inspired the creation of Freedom Paws Wellness.

## Our Mission

Freedom Paws Wellness is a **mission-driven pet wellness platform** that combines:

- **Holistic education** — ten tokenized wellness protocols  
- **AI-powered Vision Transformer (ViT) diagnostics** — photo and video guidance for dog parents  
- **Freedom Paws ID** — biometric identity for unchipped dogs and shelter reunion  
- **Community impact** — give-back to no-kill shelters and veteran dog programs  
- **XRPL-native commerce** — protocol access via Xaman wallet (XRP / RLUSD)

We believe every dog deserves freedom — freedom to move comfortably, freedom from anxiety, freedom from digestive distress, and the freedom to live a long, vibrant life.

## What We Are Not

- **Not veterinary medical advice** — always consult your licensed veterinarian  
- **Not an investment product** — protocol tokens grant **educational access only**  
- **Not a replacement for emergency care** — urgent signs require prompt professional evaluation

# Freedom Paws ID & Adoption Network

## The problem we solve

**40–60%** of shelter intakes are unchipped, unreadable, or unregistered. Scanners help when chips work — they cannot reunite the dog that never had one.

## Freedom Paws ID — Track 1 (biometric, live in pilot)

Phone-camera **Vision Transformer** identity from eyes, face, body, posture, and gait — the same AI stack as wellness ViT.

```
Owner enrolls at home → Shelter photographs found dog → Staff review match → Owner notified → Reunion
```

- **Human approval required** before any owner contact  
- **Privacy by design** — no owner PII on public pages  
- **Pilot regions:** Tennessee · target **Oct 1, 2026** full pilot  

## Track 2 — Microchip scanner kits *(in development)*

Retail scanner bundles for shelters and clinics — integrates with AAHA lookup UX. Hardware QA in progress.

# Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Freedom Paws Wellness?

A mission-driven **everything app for dogs** — holistic protocols, AI wellness guidance (ViT), biometric ID, adoption tools, Photo Booth, and XRPL token shop — on any phone via PWA.

### Are the protocols veterinary advice?

**No.** All content is **educational only**. Always consult your licensed veterinarian before changing diet, supplements, or care plans.

### What is ViT Diagnostics?

**Vision Transformer** AI that analyzes photos and short videos with your symptom description to suggest wellness indications and matching holistic protocols. It does **not** diagnose disease.

### What is Freedom Paws ID?

Biometric identity for dogs using phone camera — especially valuable for **unchipped** dogs. Shelters match found dogs to enrolled members with **human staff approval**.

### What are MPTs and Dynamic NFTs?

**MPTs (Multi-Purpose Tokens)** on XRPL provide protocol access with built-in royalty routing for community impact. **Dynamic NFTs** *(roadmap)* are updatable pet wellness records. Both are **utility tokens** — not investments.

### How does the Patriotic DAO work? *(planned)*

Community stakeholders will vote via Snapshot on how impact funds are distributed to shelters, veterans, and environmental initiatives. Details published before first vote.

### How do I get started?

1. Visit [app.freedompawsinc.com](https://app.freedompawsinc.com)  
2. Add to Home Screen (PWA)  
3. Try ViT Diagnostics or browse protocols  
4. Connect Xaman wallet to purchase protocol access  

### Where does the money go?

Protocol purchases fund platform operations and **10% net give-back** — split between veteran dog organizations and no-kill shelters.

### Is my pet’s data private?

Yes. Pet photos and wellness history are stored with **owner-controlled** privacy practices. Biometric enrollment requires explicit consent. See Privacy Policy *(post-LLC)*.

### Can shelters join for free?

**Pilot partners** receive free ID and listing access during Tennessee pilot. Contact **grants@freedompawsinc.com**.

### What is ViT Pro?

A **veterinary professional** module *(in validation)* — not marketed to the public. Licensed veterinarians receive literature-cited clinical decision support reports.

### Is Freedom Paws on the App Store?

No — we use a **PWA** (Progressive Web App) for faster updates and global access without store fees. Install from your browser.

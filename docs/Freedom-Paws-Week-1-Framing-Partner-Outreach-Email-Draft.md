## Why framing is different from print POD

| | Print POD (Shutterfly, etc.) | Custom framing (Framebridge, etc.) |
|---|------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Signup** | Self-serve affiliate network | B2B email to partnerships team |
| **Approval time** | 3–14 days | 4–10 weeks |
| **Week 1 goal** | Apply + get approved | **Intro only** — start the conversation |
| **Follow-up** | 7 days | 7, 14, and 28 days |

Framing is your **premium gift line** (“ready to hang”). One live framing partner at launch beats three pending POD links.

# Install Freedom Paws on Your Phone

## No App Store · No download fee · iPhone & Android

**Visit or scan:** [https://app.freedompawsinc.com](https://app.freedompawsinc.com)

---

| **iPhone (Safari only)** | **Android (Chrome)** |
|--------------------------|----------------------|
| 1. Open **Safari** — not Facebook, Instagram, or email in-app browser | 1. Open **Chrome** on your phone |
| 2. Go to **app.freedompawsinc.com** | 2. Go to **app.freedompawsinc.com** |
| 3. Tap **Share** (square with arrow up) at the bottom | 3. Tap **menu (⋮)** or **Install app** when prompted |
| 4. Tap **Add to Home Screen** | 4. Choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app** |
| 5. Tap **Add** — paw icon on your home screen | 5. Confirm — opens like a native app |

---

### Important

- **Not in the App Store or Google Play** — install from your browser.
- **iPhone:** Must use **Safari**. In-app browsers cannot add to Home Screen.
- **Already installed?** Open from your home screen paw icon for the full app experience.

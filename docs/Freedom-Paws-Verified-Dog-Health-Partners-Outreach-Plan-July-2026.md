## 1. Founder analysis — adopt now vs later

### Verdict

**Adopt the philosophy and Wave 1 contact list now.** Defer public badge, certification claims, scaled commission links, and the annual report until **after L5** (and preferably after a small pilot of 2–3 educational partners). Soft interest emails that select brands against published standards are OK **before** live commissions — do not paste production affiliate URLs or imply FTC-cleared “may earn commission” surfaces until counsel clears Packet A disclosures.

| Adopt **now** (pre-L5 / Packet A) | Adopt **after L5** | Defer to **Phase later** (post-pilot / ~Jan 2027+) |
|----------------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Evidence-based evaluation language | Live tracked affiliate links in app | Public **Dog Health Verified** badge (pending TM) |
| Published internal evaluation framework | Safe Picks / protocol affiliate env URLs | Annual *State of Canine Health & Nutrition Report* |
| Wave 1 soft outreach (charity-aligned first) | Full FTC disclosure on all CTAs | Formal “certification” program branding |
| Three-tier model (planning + CRM tags) | Give Back Alliance commission % mechanics | Broad Wave 2–3 catalog (chews/bones at scale) |
| Selecting-them outreach email voice | Insurance/telehealth kits (existing P10) | Media push of annual report |
| CRM tags: `VerifiedCandidate`, `CommunityCandidate` | Counsel-cleared 10% give-back copy | Implying FDA/AAFCO/regulatory endorsement |

### Recommendations (non-negotiable)

1. **Trademark risk — Dog Health Verified™:** Treat as concept only. Run USPTO TESS + common-law search; ask counsel before any public use of ™. Prefer interim label: **“Freedom Paws Verified Partner”** (descriptive program name, lower TM heat) until clearance.
2. **Do not overclaim charity ties:** Only brands in §6 with documented shelter/rescue programs may be pitched as Community Partner / Give Back Alliance candidates. Limited-public-info brands can still be Verified Partners on product quality alone.
3. **Align Community Partner with FP 10% give-back:** Community Partner recognition should reinforce Freedom Paws’ **10% of eligible net → shelters + working/veteran dog orgs (50/50)** — not replace it. Optional: co-advertise that a % of *affiliate commission* also routes to named orgs (Give Back Alliance).
4. **Prioritize charity-aligned brands for Wave 1** (Honest Kitchen → … → Nordic Naturals order in §7).
5. **Badge must not imply FDA / regulatory certification:** Criteria = Freedom Paws editorial standards only. Required disclaimer: *Not a government or veterinary board certification; not a guarantee of safety or efficacy.*
6. **Timing vs affiliate legal gates:** Soft contact and LOI exploration OK now; **live commissions and public “Shop partner” CTAs after L5**. Marketing automation stays prepare-only per ACTIVATION-GATE until founder activates.

### Pilot-first sequence

```
L5 counsel clears affiliate + give-back language
  → Soft Wave 1 (10 brands, charity-aligned first)
  → 2–3 Founding / Verified pilots (education + link)
  → Safe Picks / protocol surfaces live
  → Community Partner + Give Back Alliance naming
  → Badge + annual report (Jan 2027+ if traffic/methodology ready)
```

## 2. Core positioning & mission

> Our platform is dedicated to evidence-based canine health, evaluating products based on ingredient sourcing, contaminant testing, manufacturing quality, and scientific integrity. We only recommend companies that meet our standards, and we'd like to explore a long-term affiliate and educational partnership.

**Voice rule:** We are **selecting them**, not asking for permission to be another affiliate site.

### Mission framing (use verbatim)

> Our mission is to help every dog live a longer, healthier life and to ensure more lost dogs find their way home. Every product, partnership, and subscription supports that mission by advancing canine health, empowering owners with responsible AI tools, and returning a portion of our profits to shelters and working dog organizations.

### Differentiator (pitch close)

Fewer competitors combine **responsible AI (ViT)** + **lost-dog ID** + **trusted education** + **evidence-based product evaluation** + **shelter collaboration** + **ongoing give-back**. Lead with outcomes for dogs — not “another AI pet app.”

## 3. Evaluation framework

Publish internally now; publish externally on `/wellness/partners` (or a dedicated Verified Partners page) **after L5** copy review.

| Criterion | What we look for | Disqualify signals |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Ingredient sourcing** | Traceable farms/suppliers; clear protein & additive lists | Mystery meal, opaque “meat by-product” only |
| **Contaminant testing** | Heavy metals / pathogen / mycotoxin testing disclosed or available on request | No testing story; recent unresolved recalls |
| **Manufacturing quality** | Documented facilities, lot controls, GFSI/SQF or equivalent where claimed | Repeated manufacturing defects |
| **Scientific integrity** | Claims matched to evidence; no miracle cures | Disease-treatment claims for OTC supplements |
| **Ethical sourcing** | Regenerative, welfare, sustainability narrative with substance | Greenwashing with zero documentation |
| **Community giving** | Documented shelter/rescue/working-dog support (*for Community Partner tier*) | Marketing-only “we love rescues” with no program |

**SKU rule (unchanged from Safe Picks):** Brand acceptance ≠ every SKU. Whitelist per protocol; no BHA/BHT/ethoxyquin in primary picks; no rawhide as primary chew; quarterly FDA recall audit.

## 4. Partnership tiers

Align CRM and future UI to three tiers. Cap **Founding Partner** intentionally.

| Tier | Who | Benefits (offer) | Obligations |
|------|-----|------------------|-------------|
| **Founding Partner** | ≤ ~10 companies; Wave 1 close-wins | Featured placement, expert interviews, joint webinars, early pilot access, co-branded shelter initiatives, annual impact mention | Meet full evaluation framework; educational collab (not link-only); affiliate agreement + FTC |
| **Verified Partner** | Meets published evaluation framework | Review articles, product listings, affiliate integration, educational content, trust badge (*when launched*) | Maintain standards; disclose material formula/facility changes |
| **Community Partner** | Actively supports rescues, shelters, working/service dogs (**documented**) | Recognition for social impact; Give Back Alliance eligibility; co-marketing with FP 10% story | Documented giving program; optional commission % to shelters/veteran orgs |

**Community Partner ↔ FP give-back:** Community Partner status **amplifies** Freedom Paws’ 10% give-back narrative. It does not substitute for counsel-cleared give-back math on Token Shop + affiliate net.

## 9. Badge, Give Back Alliance & annual report (phased)

### Dog Health Verified™ (Phase later — post-L5 / ~Jan 2027)

**Concept:** Badge for brands meeting published criteria (transparency, manufacturing, testing, science support, ethical sourcing, community giving for Community tier).

**Credibility caveats (required):**
- Not FDA, USDA, AAFCO, or veterinary board certification  
- Not a guarantee of product safety or clinical efficacy  
- Freedom Paws editorial / partnership standard only  
- Criteria published; inconsistent application destroys trust  

**TM:** Pending counsel + TESS. Until then use **“Freedom Paws Verified Partner”** without claiming a registered certification mark.

### Give Back Alliance

Propose that Verified / Community partners optionally dedicate a **% of Freedom Paws–attributed commission** to shelters and veteran/working-dog orgs — **in addition to** FP’s own 10% of eligible net. Pitch as measurable impact + commercial alignment. Mechanics and public copy require L5 give-back clarity + partner agreement language.

### Annual *State of Canine Health & Nutrition Report* (Phase later)

Include when methodology is ready: top foods/supplements/treats (framework-scored), ingredient trends, recall summaries, contaminant transparency notes, shelter trends, **aggregate anonymized** AI health insights, lost-dog recovery stats. Objective tone; cite sources; no pay-to-win rankings. Natural home for Impact Dashboard data (see Impact Dashboard Plan — gated similarly).

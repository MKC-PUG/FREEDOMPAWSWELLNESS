### The problem

40–60% of shelter intakes are **unchipped, unreadable, or unregistered**. Microchip scanners help when chips work — but they cannot reunite the dog that never had one.

---

### Our answer (Track 1 — live now)

**Phone-camera biometric ID** powered by the same Vision Transformer as Freedom Paws wellness diagnostics:

| Region | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Eyes | Unique markings, heterochromia |
| Face | Coat patterns, ear shape |
| Body | Size, markings, scars |
| Posture | Stance, asymmetry |
| Gait (video) | Movement signature — hard to fake |

Owners enroll at home. Shelters intake found dogs with one photo or short video. **Human staff approve matches** before any owner email.

### How it works (60 seconds)

```
Owner enrolls → Embeddings stored → Dog goes missing
       ↓
Shelter uploads found photo → Similarity search (pgvector)
       ↓
Staff reviews candidates → Approve → Owner emailed → Reunion
```

**Match threshold:** 0.72 default (founder tuning). Every approval is logged.

---

### What this is NOT

- Not a government pet license  
- Not automatic contact — **human review required**  
- Not a replacement for microchips — **Track 2 adds scanner + AAHA lookup**

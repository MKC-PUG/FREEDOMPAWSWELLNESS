## Privacy Policy

### What we collect

Account email (magic link sign-in), pet profile data you enter, ViT upload metadata, biometric enrollment media and descriptors (with consent), found-dog intake submissions, waitlist email, and usage necessary to operate the service.

### How we use data

To provide ViT analysis, Freedom Paws ID matching with human review, cloud pet sync, partner referral attribution, shelter pilot operations, and service improvements. We do not sell personal data.

### AI & vision processing

Photos and videos may be processed by third-party AI providers (e.g. OpenAI) to generate wellness descriptors and protocol suggestions. Do not upload images you do not have rights to share.

### Biometric data

Enrollment descriptors and embeddings are stored to enable lost-dog matching. You may revoke biometric enrollment in ID Settings. Revocation removes match eligibility until re-enrollment.

### Cookies & local storage

We use cookies for authentication and local storage for PWA features (e.g. protocol unlocks on device). Preview mode may limit search engine indexing.

### Third parties

We use Supabase (auth/database), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email), OpenAI (vision), and affiliate partners you choose to visit. Each has its own privacy policy.

### Your choices

Sign in to manage pets and ID settings. Email info@freedompawsinc.com to request account data deletion where applicable.

### Contact

Privacy inquiries: info@freedompawsinc.com

## MOU milestone guidance (for after L5 — Section 16 summary)

**Do not include full MOU drafting in Packet A fee.** When counsel later drafts pilot MOUs:

| Question | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| Is **3 months** enough to prove the system works? | **Yes** — with enrollment push, found-dog drills weeks 2–4, milestone-based MOU language |
| Is 3 months enough to prove scale / multi-year deals on reunion volume alone? | **No** — Jan 2027 promotion window |

**Preferred term structure:** **6-month pilot** with **90-day checkpoint**, or **3 + 3 auto-extension** when operational milestones met — **not reunion-only**.

### Milestone-based success (majority of operational + ≥1 outcome)

| # | Category | Example |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | Outcome | ≥1 documented reunion **or** high-confidence near-miss |
| 2 | Enrollment | ≥25–50 owner enrollments attributed to partner |
| 3 | Found intake | ≥10–15 found-dog intakes with completed match workflow |
| 4 | Training | ≥2 staff + ≥1 shelter_admin trained |
| 5 | Adoption directory | ≥5 live listings where applicable |
| 6 | Safety | No unauthorized PII disclosure; consent used; no off-workflow owner contact |
| 7 | E2E drill | ≥1 test drill within 30 days of go-live |

**Explicit non-goals for pilot MOU:** no multi-year exclusivity; no reunion-count SLAs; no paid fees in pilot; no Track 2 scanner obligations.

**Counsel note:** Per-shelter MOU success should not require a reunion at every org — aggregate network proof can satisfy pilot gate.

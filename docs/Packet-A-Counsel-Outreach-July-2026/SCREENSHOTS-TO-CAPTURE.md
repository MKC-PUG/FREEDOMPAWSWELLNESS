### Steps

1. Open Safari → paste the URL → go.
2. Sign in with your founder magic-link account if prompted.
3. Start or resume enrollment until you reach **Step 2 — Biometric consent** (the screen with the consent bullets and unchecked checkbox).

## Capture 4 & 5 — Live Terms + Privacy PDFs (strongly recommended)

| Filename | URL | How |
|----------|-----|-----|
| `01-terms-live.pdf` | https://app.freedompawsinc.com/terms | iPhone Safari → Share → Print → **Save as PDF** |
| `02-privacy-live.pdf` | https://app.freedompawsinc.com/privacy | Same |

### iPhone print-to-PDF steps

1. Open the URL in Safari.
2. Tap Share (square with arrow).
3. Scroll actions → **Print**.
4. Pinch-out the preview to open full PDF preview (if shown) → Share → **Save to Files**.
5. Save into Files → On My iPhone or iCloud → AirDrop to Mac → put in `Packet-A-2026-07`.

**Desktop alternate:** Chrome → open URL → `Cmd+P` → Destination **Save as PDF**.

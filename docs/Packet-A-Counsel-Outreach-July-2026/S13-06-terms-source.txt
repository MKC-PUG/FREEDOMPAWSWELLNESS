Educational wellness platform
Freedom Paws Wellness provides educational wellness information, AI-assisted pattern recognition (ViT Diagnostics), holistic protocol content, and partner referrals. We are not a veterinary clinic, do not diagnose or treat animals, and do not prescribe pharmaceutical drugs.

Not veterinary medical advice
All content, ViT outputs, and protocol recommendations are for informational purposes only. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for diagnosis, treatment, and emergency care. If your dog shows urgent signs, seek in-person veterinary care immediately.

Freedom Paws ID
Biometric enrollment requires explicit consent. Freedom Paws ID is not a government pet license. Match candidates require human review before owner contact. We do not guarantee reunion outcomes.

Affiliate & partner disclosures
Freedom Paws may earn commissions from insurance affiliates, telehealth partners, protocol affiliates, and Safe Picks product links. Partner services are provided by third parties under their own terms. Prices and coverage vary by partner.

Payments & digital access
Protocol access may be purchased via XRPL/Xaman or other payment methods when enabled. Digital access unlocks educational content on your account or device as described at checkout. Refund policies are stated at point of purchase.

Give-back commitment
Freedom Paws targets 10% of net platform proceeds to veteran dog organizations and no-kill shelters (50/50 split), subject to accounting and partner program terms.

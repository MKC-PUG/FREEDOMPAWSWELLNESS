## Flow

| Step | Where | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Studio (Production) | The cut is finished and you clicked **Hand to SuperBud**. |
| 2 | This page | A draft card appears in **Pipeline**. One card per network. |
| 3 | This page | You read the caption and the script. They must match the approved words. |
| 4 | This page | You click **Approve**. The badge becomes Ready. |
| 5 | This page | You click **Send to Buffer** once for that card. |
| 6 | Buffer | You confirm the queue card is a video. You publish. This page does not publish by itself. |

## 1. Open the page

1. Open https://app.freedompawsinc.com/ops/marketing
2. Confirm the title is **SuperBud (Distribution)**.
3. Confirm this guide is the first block under the title.
4. The badge **Blocked** means the emergency stop is on. Leave it on. It covers the automation toggles. It does not grey out **Send to Buffer**.

## 2. What this page does

This page sends. It holds social drafts, approval, and Buffer.

The picture is made on Studio (Production). Member email is closed. There is no member list, and this page does not connect a mailbox. Shelter email is not sent from here.

## 3. Read the draft

1. Open **Pipeline**.
2. Click the new card. A handoff makes one card per network you chose on Studio.
3. Read **Caption** and **Script**. If the words are not the approved slate, click **Archive**. Do not send that card.
4. Confirm the CTA is the module you filmed: `/adopt/tn`, `/id`, `/diagnostics`, `/mypets`, `/photobooth`, or `/waitlist`.
5. A health caption includes the closer. A module intro that never discusses health does not.
6. An approved Canva file shows as a link on the card. That file is not sent to Buffer.

## 4. Send

1. Click **Approve**.
2. Click **Send to Buffer** once.
3. **Send to Buffer OK** means the webhook accepted it. Open Buffer and confirm one video card, with no phone icon.
4. **Send to Buffer OK (dry-run)** means the words were stored here and Buffer did not receive them. The webhook is not set yet.
5. A red line and badge **Blocked** on the card means the webhook refused it. Read the line. Do not click a second send until the first card is fixed.
6. Repeat for the other network cards. One send per card. A second send creates a second queue card. Delete the extra in Buffer.
7. Instagram must say Reel. Facebook must say Reel. YouTube must say Short. X shows the video on the Freedom Paws queue. Leave **Publish Now** alone.
8. Skip TikTok. It is not a Buffer channel.

## 5. Counts

**Total**, **In draft**, **Needs approval**, **Approved**, **Scheduled**, **Posted**.

In draft counts drafts and cards waiting for approval. Nothing in those counts has been published. **Posted** changes only after a later update. Your confirmation is the Buffer queue.

## 6. Stop

- Do not send a shop caption, an affiliate link, a percent, a reunion rate, or a diagnosis.
- Do not turn on workflows A through I.
- Do not email a vendor, a shelter, or a member from this page.

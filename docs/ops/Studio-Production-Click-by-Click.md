## Flow

| Step | On this page | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | See what is waiting | Read the counts. |
| 2 | Choose the networks | Pick Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, or all. Skip TikTok. |
| 3 | Queue one topic | Queue one education row, or add a row from research. |
| 4 | Open the job and save the words | Read Do not say. Save the script and the caption. |
| 5 | Make the picture | ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Runway, then CapCut. |
| 6 | Canva layout, then approve it | Optional. Approve the file here, or reject it. |
| 7 | Hand the cut to SuperBud | Draft cards are created. Nothing is sent to Buffer. |

## 1. See what is waiting

Waiting on you means a job still needs research, a script, or a watch. Rendering means a HeyGen job is in flight from this desk.

## 2. Choose the networks

The choice is saved when you queue the topic. Skip **TikTok Only**. TikTok is not a Buffer channel.

## 3. Queue one topic

Click **Queue** on one row. Vendor product rows stay on the list. Queue one only after that partner is signed and the words are approved. Research can add a new row. It does not send anything.

## 4. Open the job and save the words

1. Click the new job.
2. Read **Do not say**.
3. Paste the approved script and the approved caption.
4. Click **Save**.

## 5. Make the picture

One spoken line is one talking clip.

1. **ElevenLabs.** Voice **SuperBud-FP-Designed** only. Voice ID `99s7C3P60wz7zQFwG3Ww`.
2. **HeyGen Avatar IV.** Locked still plus that audio. HeyGen does not supply the voice.
3. **Runway Gen-4.5** only when the mouth is not delivering a line.
4. **CapCut** on this Mac. 9:16. 1080×1920. Burn the words he is saying.
5. Export MP4 and upload it to the **social-videos** bucket. Copy the public https link.

Leave **Approve script and render** alone when you are cutting the clips yourself. That button starts HeyGen from this desk.

Locked stills live in `docs/brand/SuperBud-Master/frames/`. Leave every file in `not-identity/` out of HeyGen, Runway, CapCut, and Canva.

## 6. Canva layout, then approve it

This step is optional. It fills a brand template. It does not draw a new dog.

1. Click **Connect Canva** once.
2. Choose the template for the end card, the thumbnail, and the one-page sheet. Click **Save templates**.
3. Text fields are **HEADLINE**, **CTA**, and **MODULE**. The sheet may also have **BODY**. An image field named **STILL** receives the locked plate.
4. Click **Make end card**, **Make thumbnail**, or **Make one-page sheet**.
5. Click **Approve Canva layout**, or **Reject**.

An unapproved file blocks step 7. An approved file is linked on the SuperBud card. Buffer does not receive it.

## 7. Hand the cut to SuperBud

1. Click **Hand to SuperBud**.
2. The button changes to **On SuperBud**.
3. Open SuperBud (Distribution). Continue at step 2 there.

## Stop

- Do not state a percent, a reunion rate, a diagnosis, or “AI vet.”
- A health line still ends by telling the owner to call their own veterinarian.

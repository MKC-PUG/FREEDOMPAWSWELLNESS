## 1. Executive summary

Freedom Paws Wellness is a US-first, phone-first company: a daily habit for dog owners (ViT education, protocols, SuperBud Photo Booth, Freedom Paws ID) and, on a separate track, VitProScan for clinics. Shelters are the distribution wedge. Scanner kits open doors. Membership, protocols, affiliates, and clinic subscriptions are how the company gets paid.

**Where the company is:** the product is largely built. The company may not sell until Bass writes the pilot clearance, the EIN exists, and a bank account is in the LLC’s name.

**How this update changes the May 30, 2026 plan:**

- The launch clock starts the week the clearance PDF is saved, then runs **12 weeks to a soft launch** (public mode, one Tennessee partner, one real payment). It does not restart a 15-week rebuild of the PWA.
- **Monitor stays in the vision and leaves the launch gate.** Cloud relay waits until a shelter workflow is repeatable and counsel has refreshed the privacy terms.
- **No lifetime price.** Founding members get 12 months at $79, then renew at $99. A lifetime lock would cap the consumer line for the entire five-year window.
- **Budgets use the June 2026 product-line rows, shifted.** The first selling half is April–September 2027 at the old “Y0.5” dollars. The May membership sheet (about 12,800 paying members and about $1.8 million in one year) is not the budget.
- The June headline “about $2.22 million over five selling years” **does not equal the sum of its own period rows.** Those rows sum to **$2,417,600**. This file and the CSV use the row sum.

**Planning years** close on September 30 so each year is two clean half-years. The five-year anniversary of this update is October 23, 2031, twenty-three days after Planning Year 5 ends. The half that starts October 1, 2031 is shown as the run-rate entering the sixth year.

**Base case through September 30, 2031:** about **$1.91 million** revenue.  
**Tenth selling half (October 2031–March 2032):** **$512,500**, about **$1.03 million** annualized, with about **1,300** paying members and **320** clinics.  
**Scale ceiling on this stack** (aggressive case, only if the triggers in section 8 hit): about **$1.65 million** annualized early in 2032. A larger company needs a sales team and capital this plan does not assume.

## 4. Pet room monitor — kept, and taken off the launch gate

### Member promise (when it ships)

> “Point an inexpensive Wi-Fi camera at your pet’s room. Open Freedom Paws → Monitor → see live video when you’re away.”

### Hardware path (unchanged, still the right v1)

| Tier | Cameras | How it connects | In v1? |
|------|---------|-----------------|--------|
| **A** | Wyze Cam v3/v4 on RTSP firmware, or a cheap ONVIF camera | A home bridge or a Freedom Paws relay turns RTSP into HLS/WebRTC | Yes, when the module is scheduled |
| **B** | Cameras with a snapshot or MJPEG URL | Simple image polling | Fallback |
| **C** | Ring, Nest, Arlo | Closed partner APIs | No |

Browsers cannot play raw RTSP. v1 is documented Wyze RTSP plus an optional relay for members who opt in. No cloud recording in v1. Terms: the member owns the camera, the camera covers a space they are allowed to record, and Freedom Paws does not sell video.

**Schedule:** architecture spike after the soft launch has one live partner. Member-ready one-camera view only with a counsel pass on the privacy text. Price the relay at **$4.99 / month** so a free member cannot run up video cost. Until then the app may keep a setup guide and nothing else.

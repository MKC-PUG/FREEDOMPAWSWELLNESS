## 1. Executive summary

**The question:** Is a 3-month pilot (Oct–Dec 2026) enough time to get a reunion story — and enough to convert pilot partners into longer agreements?

**The answer:**

| Question | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| Is 3 months enough to **prove the system works**? | **Yes** — with aggressive enrollment, early found-dog drills, and milestone-based MOU language |
| Is 3 months enough to **prove scale** or lock multi-year deals on reunion volume alone? | **No** — that is the Jan 2027 promotion window by design |

Three months is **enough for proof, not for scale.** Our internal roadmaps deliberately set a **low reunion bar for the pilot** (≥1 documented reunion or validated near-miss) and a **higher bar for promotion** (5+ reunions, 15+ shelters). That is the right product strategy — but it creates a **partnership risk** if MOUs treat 90 days as reunion-or-fail.

**Recommendation:** Structure every shelter MOU as **6 months** with a **3-month checkpoint**, or as **3 + 3 auto-extension** when operational milestones are met — not reunion-only. Front-load enrollment, run found-dog drills in weeks 2–4, and separate **pilot success** from **renewal / annual partnership** criteria.

## 3. Why 3 months feels short

Your concern is valid. Reunions are not a software-only outcome — they require a **flywheel on both sides.**

### Side A — Enrolled pets (supply of matchable identity)

- Adoption handoff prompt (strong at handoff, soft at listing publish — TN Pilot Spec §7)
- Adoption events with **50 free enrollments per shelter** (ID Roadmap §5.4)
- Owner uptake: 9-step enroll wizard with explicit biometric consent
- Geographic density: enrolled pets must overlap where strays enter intake

### Side B — Found-dog intakes (demand for matches)

- Staff trained on found intake workflow (`/id/found` → `/id/match`)
- Real stray volume at partner shelter
- Quality photos/video (lighting, gait, minimal blur — Shelter Onboarding §3)
- Human review before owner contact (correct policy, adds days)

### What compresses the window

| Factor | Effect on 90-day reunion odds |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| **Training ramp** | Week 1–2 often lost to MOU, accounts, Zoom training |
| **Luck / timing** | A reunion may land in month 4–5 even when workflow is perfect |
| **Seasonality** | Stray volume varies; one quiet month ≠ product failure |
| **Enrollment lag** | Listing live ≠ owner enrolled; handoff moment is critical |
| **Match threshold** | Default 0.72 similarity — founder tuning pending; near-misses still prove value |

### Planning math (internal projections)

With ~**500 enrollments** in pilot geography and active found intakes, roadmaps imply **1–3 reunions in the first ~6 months** if operations run as designed. In **3 months**, **zero reunions** is possible even when the build is correct — that is the core risk for MOU renewal if partners judge reunion count alone.

## 4. Is 3 months enough? — Decision table

| Goal | 3 months enough? | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Prove **technical E2E** (found → match → alert) | **Yes** | Achievable with drills + 1 live or near-miss |
| Prove **≥1 public reunion story** | **Maybe** | Plan for it; do not guarantee in MOU |
| Prove **adoption / enroll habit** at shelter | **Tight** | Requires day-one handoff prompt + events |
| Hit **50+ enrollments** pilot geo | **Achievable** | Needs 6 orgs × handoffs + 1–2 events |
| Hit **20–25 found intakes** | **Achievable** | Needs trained staff + drill culture |
| Prove **ROI / renewal-worthy volume** | **No** | Jan 2027+ metrics (5+ reunions, 20 shelters) |
| **Solidify** multi-year municipal contract | **Unlikely** on reunions alone in 90 days | Use operational milestones + extension |

**Do not** ask shelters to judge the pilot **only** on reunion count in 90 days. Judge on **operational + outcome milestones** (Section 5).

## 5. How to structure the pilot so it doesn't fail at 90 days

### 5.1 MOU milestone language (not reunion-only)

Define pilot success as **any combination** of measurable operational and outcome criteria:

| Category | Example milestone |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Outcome** | ≥1 documented reunion **or** ≥1 approved near-miss (high-confidence match, verified in person) |
| **Enrollment** | ≥25–50 owner enrollments attributed to that shelter |
| **Intake** | ≥Y found intakes logged with match workflow completed |
| **Operations** | Staff trained; shelter_admin match review live |
| **Safety** | 0 PII leaks; counsel-approved consent flow live |
| **Adoption network** | Live listings; adopted-within-72h KPI met |

**Automatic extension trigger (placeholder for counsel):**  
*If operational milestones A–C are met at the 90-day checkpoint, the pilot extends to **6 months** from start date without renegotiation, even if a public reunion story is still pending.*

### 5.2 Found-dog drills early (weeks 2–4)

Do not wait for a real stray.

1. Shelter uploads practice intake (staff dog or archived photo with consent).
2. System returns top-5 candidates.
3. `shelter_admin` runs approve/reject workflow.
4. Test owner alert fires (test account).

**Proof the system works** before a reunion happens. ID Roadmap outreach sequence targets **first found-dog test drill by Day 30** post-MOU.

### 5.3 Front-load enrollment

| Tactic | Source |
|--------|--------|
| **50 free owner enrollments** per shelter per adoption event | ID Roadmap §5.4 |
| **Strong prompt at adoption handoff** (email/QR) — not optional at listing publish | TN Pilot Spec §7 |
| **Nashville-corridor privates (P1–P3)** + **MACC (M2)** for frequency while founder is Nashville-based | TN Pilot Spec §2 |
| **Public directory traffic** → adopt hub → handoff | Marketing Network Plan |

More enrolled pets = better odds a real found dog matches inside 90 days.

### 5.4 Term structure — 6 months vs 3 + 3

| Term | Pros | Cons |
|------|------|------|
| **3 months** | Easier municipal yes; faster “decision” calendar | Reunion roulette; false negative on renewal |
| **6 months** | Better reunion odds; same calendar year as Oct pilot start | Slightly slower formal “renewal” conversation |
| **3 + 3 extension** | Compromise; checkpoint at 90 days | Requires clear, objective extension triggers in MOU |

**Founder recommendation:** **6-month pilot** with **3-month checkpoint** (metrics review and transparency report — not termination unless operational failure or safety breach).

Alternative acceptable: **3-month initial term** with **automatic 3-month extension** when milestone checklist ≥80% complete.

### 5.5 Separate pilot from renewal

| Document | Purpose | Success criteria |
|----------|---------|------------------|
| **Pilot MOU / LOI** | Prove workflow, safety, enroll habit, E2E match path | Milestones (Section 5.1) |
| **Renewal / annual MOU** | Ongoing partnership, grant co-marketing, optional paid tier | Reunion count, adoption traffic, listing accuracy, case study rights |

**Do not** tie the **first** MOU’s renewal solely to “we got a reunion in Q4.” A partner who completes drills, enrollments, and intakes but misses a reunion by 30 days should **extend**, not exit.

## 6. What to say / not say to partners

### Say

- “We’re measuring pilot success by **trained staff, enrollment at handoff, found-intake workflow, and at least one reunion or validated near-miss** — with quarterly transparency.”
- “Freedom Paws ID **adds** to microchips and QR tags — it covers the **unchipped gap** shelters see every intake shift.” (Shelter Onboarding §8)
- “**Human review** before any owner contact — no automated PII exposure.”
- “**No cost to the city** in pilot; optional biometric tool alongside adoption directory.” (Marketing Network Plan §5D)
- “Pilot extends automatically when operational milestones are met — we’re not asking you to bet on one reunion in 90 days.”

### Do not say

- “We’ll have reunions every month.”
- “Match rate guarantees” or specific similarity-score promises to the public.
- “Shelters already agreed” before MOU is signed (Legal Counsel Playbook).
- “Government pet license” or language implying municipal endorsement beyond pilot scope.
- “Phones read microchips” (Track 2 hardware is Jan 2027+).

### 9.1 Milestone checklist (pilot success)

Parties agree the Freedom Paws ID pilot will be evaluated against the following **objective milestones**. Meeting **a majority** of operational milestones (items 2–6) **and** at least one outcome milestone (item 1 or 7) constitutes **successful pilot completion**:

1. **Outcome:** ≥1 documented reunion **or** ≥1 staff-verified high-confidence near-miss (match approved, owner contacted, pet status confirmed).
2. **Enrollment:** ≥[25–50] Freedom Paws ID owner enrollments attributed to Partner during the pilot term.
3. **Found intake:** ≥[10–15] found-dog intakes submitted through the Freedom Paws shelter portal with completed match workflow.
4. **Training:** ≥[2] shelter staff and ≥[1] shelter_admin completed Freedom Paws training (live or recorded).
5. **Adoption directory:** Partner maintains ≥[5] live adoption listings (where applicable) and marks adoptions within 72 hours per listing protocol.
6. **Safety & compliance:** No unauthorized owner PII disclosure; biometric consent flow used for all enrollments; Partner does not contact owners outside approved match workflow.
7. **E2E drill:** ≥1 found-dog **test drill** completed (intake → candidate list → review → test alert) within 30 days of go-live.

### 9.2 90-day checkpoint

At **90 days** from go-live, Freedom Paws will deliver a written checkpoint report covering milestones 1–7. Partners will meet (call or written response) within **14 days** to review metrics.
